Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Maligant Group (Pty) Ltd ("Maligant Group", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit maligantgroup.co.za or get in touch with us. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

Maligant Group (Pty) Ltd, Sandton, Gauteng, South Africa, is the responsible party for your information. For any privacy question or request, email maligantgroup@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you contact us by WhatsApp, email, phone or social media: your name, contact details and whatever you tell us about your project or booking.
  • When you browse the site: our hosting and video providers automatically receive technical details such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages or videos you load. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure.

The site has no sign-up forms or accounts, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, quote, plan and deliver your project.
  • To keep the website running, secure and fast.
  • To keep records we are required to keep by law (for example, invoices).

We do not sell your personal information. We only send you marketing if you have asked for it, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

Only with trusted service providers who help us run the site and our business, and only as far as they need it:

  • Website hosting and video delivery providers, so the site and our work can load for you.
  • Our advertising partner, only if you accept cookies (see below).
  • The messaging and email services that carry the messages you choose to send us.

Some of these providers store data outside South Africa. Where that happens, we rely on providers that are bound by data-protection laws or agreements that give protection comparable to POPIA. Videos you open on other platforms are governed by those platforms' own privacy policies.

Cookies and tracking

With your permission, we use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta Platforms (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). It sets cookies that tell us which of our Facebook and Instagram ads bring people to the site, which pieces of our work, YouTube links and booking buttons people tap, and when someone taps to contact us by WhatsApp, email or phone. Meta may use this to show our ads to people who have visited the site, or to people with similar interests. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.

The Meta Pixel only loads if you tap Accept on our cookie notice. If you tap No thanks, it does not load and the site works exactly the same. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of our home page, or by clearing your browser's data for this site. You can also manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

We do not use any other advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project information only as long as we need it for the reasons above, or as long as the law requires, and then delete it or make it anonymous.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections across the site and limited access to our business accounts.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct or delete it, object to us using it, or stop direct marketing. Email maligantgroup@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
